ZEVIQ is an international youth community empowering students through debate, public speaking, leadership, diplomacy, innovation and critical thinking. We prepare the next generation of confident communicators and ethical leaders capable of creating positive global impact.

Discover ZEVIQ Become a Member

Our Mission

ZEVIQ empowers teenagers to become confident speakers, critical thinkers, and responsible leaders through debate, public speaking, leadership development, and meaningful collaboration. We strive to create opportunities that inspire curiosity, respect, and lifelong learning.

Our Vision

To become one of the world's leading youth debate organizations, connecting students across cultures, encouraging respectful dialogue, and preparing the next generation of changemakers.

Our Core Values

🎤 Confidence

Helping every student speak with clarity and courage.

🧠 Critical Thinking

Developing the ability to analyze, question and reason.

🤝 Respect

Encouraging open-minded discussions and respectful disagreement.

🌍 Global Citizenship

Connecting young people across schools, cities and countries.
